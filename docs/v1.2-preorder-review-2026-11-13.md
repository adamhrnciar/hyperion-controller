## Checks completed from recovered evidence

- **DRC and parity:** Konnect `run_drc`, KiCad 10.0.8, saved file, zone refill enabled:
  zero design-rule violations, unconnected items and schematic-parity findings. The older
  `fab/drc_v1.2.json` remains a dated 2026-11-02 report; it is not presented as a fresh run.
  That report lists five ignored checks: missing courtyard, track not centred on via, tuning
  profile geometry, footprint filters and footprint/pad type mismatch. A clean configured run
  does not validate those omitted assembly/library checks; review their rationale at release.
- **ERC:** zero errors, one warning in `/IMU_IO/`: U6 pin 2 SDx is bidirectional and tied to
  GND/PWR_FLAG. The LSM6DSV32X unused-pin guidance permits SDx/SCx tied to GND when analog-hub/Qvar
  functions are disabled. This is an understood pin-type warning, not a zero-warning ERC result.
- **Gerbers:** prior Konnect re-exported copper, mask, silkscreen, outline and drills, then both
  paste layers separately. Geometry matched all corresponding archive members after removing
  generation/date metadata. Both existing ZIPs are identical. The saved PCB predates the archive;
  no regeneration was needed on resumption. The separate temporary job files listed only their
  selected layers; the candidate archive job correctly lists all nine fabrication layers.
- **Drills/outline:** 109 plated drill coordinates, all 0.3 mm; NPTH file is intentionally empty.
  Outline is closed, 34 × 28 mm, R1 corners, X=100…134 and Y=-128…-100 in Gerber coordinates.
  The job's 34.05 × 28.05 bounding box includes the 0.05 mm outline stroke, not a larger board.
- **Stackup:** two 35 µm copper layers, 1.51 mm FR-4 dielectric and 10 µm mask each side give
  the recorded 1.6 mm stack. Design minima are 0.2 mm track/clearance, 0.3 mm drill, 0.6 mm via
  diameter (0.15 mm annulus), and 0.5 mm hole-to-hole clearance. PCBWay's capability page fetched
  on 2026-11-13 lists 0.1 mm track/space, 0.15 mm CNC drill, 0.15 mm annulus, 1 oz copper and
  1.6 mm thickness within range. This establishes nominal capability, not assembly-process approval.
- **BOM/placement:** 34 line items, 33 unique LCSC IDs, 63 placements per board; 252 placements
  across four boards. Every assembled reference has a matching centroid entry, schematic LCSC ID,
  footprint basename and board position/rotation/side. Translation is X minus 100, Y plus 128;
  rotations agree modulo 360°. The 27 exclusions are C3 and TP1–TP26. Five MPN text differences
  are manufacturer suffixes or LED aliases (D1, D2, D3, D5, Q1), not different LCSC parts.
- **Assembly sides:** the generic manufacturing audit counted 70 front/20 back footprints and
  incorrectly inferred dual-side assembly. All 20 back footprints are bare solder pads; all
  63 assembled parts are top-side. Its eight proximity warnings concern a test pad and/or an
  opposite-side part: TP16/TP22, R5/TP24, R12/TP9, C24/TP12, R1/TP19, TP15/TP26, R10/TP11,
  C8/TP8. They do not establish top-side component collisions. They still matter for wiring access.
- **Electrical orientation:** exported nets agree with D1 cathode to protected VBAT, D2/D3
  cathodes on pad 1, LED cathodes on pad 1, and L1 winding-start pad 1 to VREG_LX. U2/U3 VREF is
  3.3 V, R13/R14 are 1.5 kΩ, and their power/ground/output nets agree. Q1's project symbol uses
  logical G/S/D numbering on a custom multi-pad footprint; do not substitute by package name alone.

## Outstanding release blockers

1. **Konnect verification unavailable.** Reconnect it, bind the final saved project, run complete
   ERC/DRC with parity and zone refill, verify manufacturing/assembly coverage, and record hashes.
   Do not turn a generic `READY` tool verdict into an order approval: it did not resolve the
   process, sourcing or footprint issues below.
2. **Custom footprint/process sign-off incomplete** (`hardware/hyperion.pretty/`, U1/U6/Q1).
   Recovered pin numbering/pitch agree with catalogue footprints, but the geometries are not
   identical. U1 uses 0.80 × 0.19 mm peripheral pads; Raspberry Pi's recommended pattern uses
   0.875 × 0.20 mm. Its 3.4 mm exposed pad and 0.4 mm pitch agree. U6 uses 0.625 × 0.29 mm
   side pads versus 0.50 × 0.28 mm in the EasyEDA comparison. That comparison links an unrelated
   IIS3DWB source and is not an authoritative LSM6DSV32X land-pattern approval. Confirm actual
   manufacturer drawings, pin-1 orientation, Q1 package-to-logical-pad mapping and assembler
   stencil acceptance. These are design/process choices; no pad sizes were silently changed.
3. **Thermal via treatment undecided** (`fab/pcbway/ASSEMBLY_NOTES.md`, Assembly; U1/U2/U3).
   U1 has nine exposed-pad holes and four 1.32 mm square paste windows, about 60% nominal exposed-pad
   coverage before holes. U2/U3 add two thermal vias each. Select and qualify fill/cap or tenting,
   paste treatment and void inspection; ordinary tenting is not proven equivalent. Solder wicking,
   poor ground attachment or excessive voids can prevent startup or cause early thermal shutdown.
4. **Y1 sourcing for four boards unconfirmed** (`fab/pcbway/hyperion-v1.2-pcbway-bom.csv`, Y1).
   Recovered pcbparts catalogue stock is one ABM8-272-T3, with minimum order 23. Four boards need
   four plus attrition. Separate Mouser lookup reported 8,213, but no stock was reserved or supplier
   allocation confirmed. Keep the exact 12 MHz/10 pF/3225-4 device unless an alternative's load,
   ESR, drive level, pad map and startup are reviewed. No crystal can mean no normal boot/USB.
   U1 and L1 had stock 1,299/283 but catalogue minimum orders 8/26; resolve procurement minimums.
5. **Surface finish not fixed** (`fab/pcbway/ASSEMBLY_NOTES.md`, PCB specification;
   ZIP `hyperion-job.gbrjob`). Job metadata says `Finish: None`, while notes recommend ENIG and
   permit lead-free HASL only as a fallback. Fix the quote to the chosen finish and qualify
   flatness/paste for 0.4 mm QFN and LGA pads. Do not infer bare-copper approval from the metadata.

## Other findings and first-power requirements

- **C3 DNP metadata:** exported schematic BOM has empty DNP/exclude flags despite DNP in its
  value and an electrolytic MPN on a 1206 placeholder footprint. The reviewed PCBWay BOM and
  centroid correctly omit it. Correct flags through Konnect before automatic re-export; never
  place that electrolytic as a 1206 SMD part. Assembly notes now make the frozen exclusions explicit.
- **C3 before battery tests:** owner must hand-wire 100 µF/35 V, positive to protected VBAT,
  short leads and strain relief, before 4S or any battery hot-plug test. Initial power comes from
  a slowly ramped current-limited bench supply. Ceramic-only input has not qualified VM ringing
  or the DRV8231A ramp-rate limit. See `firmware/BRINGUP.md`, section 3.
- **U6 symbol labels:** exported netlist calls pins 10/11 NC, but the part's actual names are
  OCS_Aux/SDO_Aux. The manufacturer permits them left unconnected with auxiliary functions unused;
  current wiring is acceptable in that mode. Correct names through Konnect; keep firmware from
  enabling the unused auxiliary/Qvar functions.
- **DRV8231A current/heat:** 1.47 A is the shared per-channel regulation threshold, not measured
  continuous capability. The 0.6 Ω bridge estimate gives about 1.3 W at that current; package and
  PCB thermal behaviour need physical testing. Task #20 remains open. Fit motors for voltage/load;
  no 3 A burst or beetleweight-drive rating is claimed.
- **5 V budget:** the nominal 1 A buck feeds receiver, servo outputs and 3.3 V rail. A servo stall
  can brown out the MCU. Measure load transients; choose external servo power if needed. Surface
  wire pads need mechanical strain relief for combat. Neither issue was changed by this review.
- **Firmware programming:** require RP2354A with internal flash, not a plain RP2350A. A blank
  board may enumerate its ROM bootloader but cannot operate as a robot controller until flashed.
  No functional factory test or programming service was assumed in the assembly package.
- **Old claims corrected:** README no longer says ERC has zero warnings or calls this package
  ready. Motor section distinguishes regulation from continuous rating and sensing from planned
  transmitter telemetry. Stale zero-stock U1 warning was replaced with dated catalogue evidence.

## Firmware review and validation

The pre-existing `rc_input.c` change timestamps frames with `time_us_64()`, snapshots the 64-bit
time under disabled interrupts, and saturates reported millisecond age at `UINT32_MAX`. It
prevents a lost receiver frame looking fresh again after the 32-bit microsecond timer wraps
at 71.6 minutes. `rc_pwm.c` independently timestamps each channel's last valid pulse in 64 bits,
so a dead CH2 wire cannot become valid again while CH1 keeps sending. Pulse-width subtraction
can remain 32-bit because valid pulse widths are bounded to 800–2200 µs.

The recovered simulator runs the real firmware with modelled SDK peripherals. Its Makefile now
also depends on nested `include/hardware/*.h` and `include/pico/*.h`, so SDK-stub changes rebuild
the executables. The historical 34-mutant claim is explicitly labelled as unreproduced here.

- `make -B -C firmware/test`: 88 checks, zero failures, ASan/UBSan and strict warnings.
- `make -C firmware/sim PIOASM=<built pioasm>`: 77 scenarios across 13 variants, zero failures,
  including SBUS/CRSF/PWM, arming, watchdog, dropout and both stale-input wrap regressions.
- Recompile with original `2923c25` `rc_input.c`: `timer_wrap_after_link_loss` fails as expected.
  Separately restore original `rc_pwm.c`: `pwm_timer_wrap_dead_channel` fails as expected.
- Fresh ARM builds: all eight default SBUS modes, plus drive with CRSF and PWM. Pico SDK 2.3.1,
  Arm GCC 16.2.0, CMake 4.4.4, Ninja 1.13.2; zero compiler warnings/errors.
- Fresh UF2/ELF outputs are under `firmware/build/review-sbus/`, `review-crsf/`, `review-pwm/`.
  UF2 dates are **2026-11-13 10:04:49–10:07:00 UTC**; hashes and sizes are in
  `fab/review-2026-11-13/firmware-validation.json`. Existing root `firmware/build/*.uf2` files
  remain dated **2026-10-31** and were not used as evidence for this build.

No board was powered or flashed. Simulation does not validate power transients, current chopping,
thermal shutdown, USB enumeration, oscillator startup, interrupt latency or combat durability.
