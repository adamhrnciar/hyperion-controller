# Hyperion v1.2 – PCB fabrication and assembly notes for PCBWay

Hyperion is an antweight robot controller (RP2354A MCU, 2x DRV8231A H-bridges, buck + LDO, IMU).

**Candidate package, not released for production.** The
[2026-11-13 pre-order review](../../docs/v1.2-preorder-review-2026-11-13.md) records the remaining
assembly-process, sourcing and verification decisions. Do not start fabrication or assembly
until those items are closed.

## PCB specification

- Size: 34.0 x 28.0 mm, rounded corners (1 mm radius), 1 board per design
- Layers: 2
- Material: FR-4
- Thickness: 1.6 mm
- Copper: 1 oz (35 µm) outer layers
- Minimum track / spacing: 0.2 mm / 0.2 mm
- Minimum drill: 0.3 mm (vias 0.3 mm drill / 0.6 mm pad, including vias in the U1 thermal pad)
- Solder mask: both sides; silkscreen: both sides (back side carries the Hyperion and PCBWay logos and the test-pad labels)
- Surface finish: **ENIG recommended** (flat pads for the 0.4 mm-pitch QFN-60 U1 and the LGA-14 U6).
  HASL lead-free is acceptable only if ENIG is not possible.
  The Gerber job metadata currently says `Finish: None` (unspecified); the final quote must
  explicitly identify the approved finish. Do not interpret that field as approval for bare copper.

## Assembly

- **Single-side SMT assembly, top side only.** No parts on the bottom side, no through-hole parts.
- **Stencil required** (top side). Paste layer is included in the Gerbers.
- U1 (QFN-60, 0.4 mm pitch, exposed pad with vias), U2/U3 (WSON-8 with exposed pad) and U6 (LGA-14):
  please check for bridging and voids (X-ray if available).
- U6 (LSM6DSV32X) is moisture sensitive; please follow MSL handling.
- **Via-in-pad process remains undecided:** U1 has nine 0.3 mm holes in its exposed pad;
  U2 and U3 have two thermal vias each. Confirm the quoted fill/cap or qualified tenting process,
  stencil treatment and void inspection before assembly. Ordinary tenting has not been qualified
  as equivalent to resin fill/cap on this design. Do not remove the thermal vias.
- These boards have no programmed/tested firmware release. Firmware loading and
  [physical bring-up](../../firmware/BRINGUP.md) are separate acceptance steps.

## Do not place (DNP)

| Designator | Reason |
|------------|--------|
| C3 | 100 µF electrolytic, hand-wired by the customer to the 1206 pads. Do not place anything. Not in BOM or centroid. |
| TP1 – TP26 | Bare copper test/solder pads (no component). Not in BOM or centroid. TP3–TP12 and TP17–TP26 are on the bottom side. |

C3 is marked DNP in its value text, but its schematic DNP/exclude-from-BOM attributes are not set.
For this package the 63-placement PCBWay BOM and centroid plus the DNP table above are authoritative;
do not generate a placement for C3 from the generic schematic BOM. Correct the schematic attributes
through Konnect before the next automatic assembly export.

The customer must fit C3 (100 µF, 35 V electrolytic, positive lead to pad 1 = protected VBAT)
before any 4S test or battery-pack hot-plug test on any cell count. Keep leads short and provide
strain relief. A slow-ramping, current-limited bench supply is the initial bring-up source;
the all-ceramic input alone does not qualify hot-plug transients or the DRV8231A VM ramp rate.

## Polarity / orientation (please check carefully)

- **L1 (3.3 µH Abracon AOTA-B201610S3R3-101-T): the inductor has a polarity/winding-start mark.
  The mark must be on pad 1 (VREG_LX, the pad nearest U1).** A silkscreen dot is printed next to
  pad 1. The RP2350/RP2354 datasheet requires this orientation for the internal core regulator.
- D1 (SMF18A, **unidirectional** TVS): cathode band = pad 1 = VBAT side.
- D2 (BZT52C12 Zener), D3 (B5819W Schottky): cathode band = pad 1.
- D4 (red LED), D5 (green LED): cathode = pad 1.
- U1, U2, U3, U4, U5, U6, Q1, Y1: pin 1 per silkscreen pin-1 marker / footprint.

## Centroid file

- Units: mm. Origin (0, 0) = lower-left corner of the board outline, X to the right, Y up, viewed from the top.
- Rotation: degrees, counter-clockwise, KiCad footprint convention (KiCad standard library footprints,
  IPC-7351 zero orientation). Please verify against the silkscreen and the notes above.
- Footprint origins are the part body centres.
- In the Gerber coordinates the board's lower-left corner is at (100.0, -128.0) mm, if you need to align
  the centroid with the Gerbers.
